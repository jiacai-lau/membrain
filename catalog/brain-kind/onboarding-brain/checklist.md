## Why this form exists

The client asked to verify by a date (or refund). This is the written checklist.

**How acceptance works**

1. The client tests each row themselves. Tick **Accept** only after they have checked it.
2. Tick **Not accept** for anything still open. Those stay in the Exceptions table — they do **not** redefine go-live for the Accept rows.
3. When an authorised signatory signs Section B, the client **accepts that the Accept rows are complete** and that the product is **go-live for those accepted items** as of the Sign-off date.
4. After sign-off, the client will not reopen an Accept row as a reason to unwind go-live or demand a full refund for work already accepted. Open Not-accept / Exceptions items stay open and get a target date.
5. This form does **not** by itself delete data or trigger a deposit refund. Refund talk only applies to items left **Not accept** or listed as Exceptions, under whatever commercial terms were already agreed.

Export a signed PDF (or a chat reply that pastes the signature block) and file it. A typed name on an editable file alone is weak.
